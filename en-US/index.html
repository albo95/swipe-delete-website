How it works

Four steps. No learning curve.

  1. Allow Photos access

    Open SwipeDelete and grant access to your library. The analysis stays on your iPhone.

  2. Pick where to start

    Start with Smart Suggestions, filter by Photos, Videos or Screenshots, by year and month, or sort by size.

  3. Swipe and decide

    Left to delete, right to keep. Made a mistake? Undo your last decision with one tap.

  4. Review and confirm

    Check the Trash grid, restore anything you want back, then confirm — iOS asks one last time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete a lot of photos quickly on iPhone?

With SwipeDelete you swipe through photos and videos like cards: left to delete, right to keep. Each decision takes a second, and you can undo the last one at any time. Learn more

Can I get back a photo I deleted by mistake?

Yes. Everything you swipe left lands in the in-app Trash first, where you can restore single items or everything. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and iOS asks for its own confirmation too. Learn more

Does SwipeDelete find duplicate photos and screenshots?

Smart Suggestions analyze your library on-device and surface screenshots, near-duplicates, unselected burst shots and low-quality photos at the top of the deck. Learn more

What is the fastest way to free up the most iPhone storage?

Sort the deck by size: the heaviest videos and files come first, so you reclaim the most gigabytes with the fewest swipes. Learn more

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud?

No. All processing happens on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. No account, no uploads. Learn more in the Privacy Policy

Is SwipeDelete a subscription?

No. You can free up to 300 MB for free; to clean without limits, a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited use on all your Apple devices. Learn more in the Terms of Use
