SwipeDelete Support

Need help? Here are the quickest answers. For anything else, write to us.

Email albosapps@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. We reply as soon as we can.

Common issues

The app can't see my photos

Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › SwipeDelete and choose Full Access. With Limited Access the app only sees the photos you selected.

I discarded a photo by mistake

Tap undo to restore your last decision, or open the Trash and restore the photo (or everything) before confirming deletion.

I confirmed deletion but I need a photo back

Check the Recently Deleted album in Photos: you can recover photos from there for about 30 days, until the album is emptied. Recovery guide.

I deleted photos but my free storage didn't go up

Deleted photos stay in “Recently Deleted.” Empty that album to reclaim the space right away. Free up space guide.

I reached the 300 MB limit

The free version lets you free up to 300 MB in total. To keep going without limits, unlock the unlimited version with a one-time purchase — no subscription. Details in the Terms of Use.

I got a new iPhone: do I have to pay again?

No. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple: you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No, the analysis happens only on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy.
