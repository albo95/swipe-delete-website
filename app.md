## Features → benefits (only these, nothing invented)
1. **Swipe to decide** — left = delete, right = keep; one second per photo/video, haptic feedback; undo the last decision at any time.
2. **Smart Suggestions** — on-device engine (Apple Vision + heuristics) finds screenshots, near-duplicates, unselected burst shots and low-quality/blurry images and puts them at the top of the deck.
3. **In-app Trash** — left-swiped items go to an internal trash shown as a grid with total size; restore single items or all, or empty with one tap. iOS asks its own confirmation before final deletion (double safety).
4. **Filter & Sort** — filter by Photos, Videos, Screenshots; by year/month; sort by date, size or random.
5. **Size Sort** — heaviest files first: most space with the fewest swipes.
6. **Lifetime Freed counter** — persistent banner with the total GB freed over time.
7. **Resume where you left off** — deck, trash and preferences saved between launches.
8. **100% on-device privacy** — no cloud upload, no account, nothing leaves the device.
9. **Monetization** — freemium: free users can free up to 300 MB lifetime; then the one-time "Unlock Unlimited" purchase (indicative price €9.99, do not publish on the site), no subscription, unlocks on all the user's Apple devices. Paywall shows "Restore Purchases".
